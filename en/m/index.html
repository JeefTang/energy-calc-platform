🗜️ I. Air compressor parameters before retrofit
For screw compressors with load/unload control, the unloading period still consumes the rated power 25%~40% (default 35%). This no-load loss is the main energy-saving potential for VFD retrofits and centralized linkage control.
⚡ II. Retrofit plan (energy-saving rate calculated automatically from the parameters below)
VFD retrofit: first eliminate unload/idle losses, then convert using the screw compressor part-load power curve——VFD unit about 20% power is fixed loss (oil pump/fan/VFD), the remainder is roughly linear with air flow, so 50% air flow requires about 60% power (specific power degradation about 20%); constant-pressure control can further reduce discharge pressure; each reduction of 0.1 MPa shaft power drops about 7%.

🪨 I. Slurry pump parameters before retrofit
Example model:4/3C-AH Centrifugal slurry pump(inlet×outlet 4×3 in, frame C / max. motor 30 kW, market designation “90 square pump“)——Nameplate rated flow rate 86.4 m³/h、head 9~52 m(example value 40 m), matched motor 30 kW; pump efficiency 48% is the slurry-condition value back-calculated from on-site measurements at the concentrator (nameplate clean-water efficiency 71%). Please modify according to the customer’s actual nameplate and measured data.

No need to guess the original pump efficiency:select “Back-calculate from measurements“, enter the current measured with a clamp meter (or the power meter reading), and the system will use ηPump = ρgQH ÷ (3.6×10⁶ × Pmeasured × ηPower × ηTransmitted) to back-calculateOriginal pump actual average efficiencyand fill it back in; if not measured, select “Use empirical value“ to get a default value by flow rate range, then correct it according to site conditions.
⚡ II. Slurry pump parameters after retrofit
Plunger slurry pump overall efficiency reference 88% (slurry duty, positive displacement); impeller/Efficiency decay after liner wear is far smaller than centrifugal type
Four common retrofit paths for slurry pumps:
① Plunger slurry pump replacement (primary recommendation)——Positive displacement work, overall efficiency in slurry duty can be taken as 85%~90% (centrifugal on-site mostly 45%~55%); valve group/seal/Plunger wetted parts life is typically 2~3 times that of centrifugal impeller and liner, and efficiency decays slowly after wear; advantages are most obvious in high-concentration, high-head duties;
② High-efficiency hydraulic model / Whole-pump replacement——Centrifugal efficiency raised from 60%~65% to 72%~78% (while also improving the material and wear life of wetted parts);
③ Variable frequency drive——When the operating flow rate is persistently below the rated value (or needs to be adjusted according to ore throughput), apply the affinity laws Q∝n, H∝n², P∝n³ to reduce speed;
④ Impeller trimming / reduce speed——When the head is persistently excessive, trim the impeller outer diameter instead of throttling with valves or recirculation.
⚠️ When reducing flow rate, verify thecritical settling velocity(generally 1.5~2.5 m/s; use the higher value for coarse particles), and do not fall below the deposition velocity, otherwise the pipe will clog.
⚠️ Sizing note：This page calculates shaft power based on P = ρ·g ·Q ·H ÷ (3.6×10⁶ × ηPump × ηTransmitted) calculation. When selecting aplunger slurry pumpthe system automatically converts the outlet differential pressure ΔP = ρ·g ·H (MPa), equivalent to P = Q × ΔP ÷ (3.6 × ηPump); the results will list it separately ΔP for model selection.

⚡ I. Motor and operating parameters (mixer-type mechanical transmission loads)
Selecting a type auto-fills efficiency and reference unit price; editable; same loss-separation model, differences in full-load efficiency and unit price
Why a separate “Mechanical transmission load“ module:Loads such as mixers, internal mixers, and screw conveyors belong toConstant torque load——The required torque is essentially constant, and power varies linearly with speed. Its energy-saving mechanism is completely different from that of fans and pumps (quadratic torque, where power varies with the cube of speed):Constant torque loads cannot save much energy through variable-frequency speed control; the real energy-saving opportunity lies in the efficiency and power factor of the motor itself under light-load conditions.

How to estimate the load factor:If a power meter is available, read it directly (actual power ÷ rated power); if only a clamp meter is available, estimate by P ≈ √3 × U × I × cosφ ; if neither is available, for mixer-type loads you can initially use 30%~50% , which is a common range.This item has the greatest impact on the result; it is recommended to verify after actual measurement.

Scope boundaries of this calculation:Only includemotor replacementefficiency gains; excludes VFD replacement, gearbox and mixer body modifications, and process optimization.Permanent magnet synchronous motors must be driven by a VFD that supports permanent magnet vector control; if the existing VFD does not support this, the investment in a high-voltage VFD must be counted separately (for this project scale, approximately 30~50 10k CNY), and the economics must then be re-evaluated.

Physical upper limit of the energy-saving rate:Motor efficiency improvement is constrained by thermodynamics and electromagnetic design;the upper limit of the energy-saving rate for a like-for-like replacement is about 2%~9%depending on the original motor’s efficiency class, aging degree and slip ring condition. Any claim that a single replacement can reach 15%~30% usually combines the benefits of “motor replacement + Variable frequency drive + system retrofit“ into one figure, or compares against a long-failed old motor. This tool does not merge such scopes.

Investment basis:Equipment price is estimated in CNY/kW, tax included; installation and commissioning cost is 15% of the equipment price (including old unit removal, lifting, alignment, electrical wiring, VFD setting). Actual prices vary with power rating, voltage class, protection class and whether an encoder is included; refer to the manufacturer’s formal quotation.

Maintenance benefit basis:Carbon brush and slip ring maintenance for wound-rotor motors (including sparking downtime losses in dusty environments) is estimated at 1.0~2.0 10k CNY/per year, already included in the payback period. Squirrel-cage motors have no such item; only electricity cost savings are counted.
